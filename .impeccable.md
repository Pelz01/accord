## Design Context

### Users

Buying and finance teams managing physical goods, suppliers confirming their invoices, and warehouse receivers recording deliveries. The job is to keep an order, what actually arrived, and what has been paid in agreement. This context comes from the creator's Accord specification and product work in this conversation.

### Brand Personality

Grounded, assured, human. Make the product feel like a capable business partner. Lead with the customer's progress; explain payment controls and AI when they help someone make a decision.

### Aesthetic Direction

The creator requested clean, tasteful fintech branding with Brex as a reference, and rejected technical descriptions, demo language, excessive labels and copied layouts. Use Accord's orange accent, warm ivory, charcoal typography and custom A monogram, original warehouse photography, Manrope/Public Sans and Bodoni accents. Use Brex, Mercury and Ramp as references for clarity and restraint, not as templates to copy.

### Design Principles

- Lead with an outcome: Keep business moving.
- Explain the product in everyday words: orders, deliveries and payments, brought together.
- Give the imagery and typography room; make each word earn its place.
- Keep identity consistent in the website, workspace, browser tab, saved app icon and shared link.
- Keep network, approval and transaction facts visible where they affect a user's decision.

The creator selected orange on 8 November 2026 to distinguish Accord from another submission. Use #ff3d00 for the mark and primary action surfaces, #ba2d00 for accessible accent text, warm ivory and charcoal elsewhere. Do not copy Brex’s logo or layouts.
