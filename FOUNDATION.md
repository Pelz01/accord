PO-TESTNET-001 then refunded its remaining 300 aUSD in transaction 5RzNhuARA7TWu7wpXwSArDG2ohmXxZemKXUfPQS9LVdZ. Its final recovery state is Closed, refunded 300000000, refund_pending 0. Both success-with-starved-callback paths and definitive refund failure/retry were exercised in real NEAR sandbox.
