## Two-wallet automatic payment release

Version 2.3.0-automatic allows the buyer to be the receiver, with optional warehouse delegation. New orders use create_automatic_order and bind automatic execution into the terms hash. Existing orders keep their original manual policy. Fixed-prefix sidecars preserve the deployed root/order/attempt layouts. The service remains separate from customer accounts; its restricted key cannot change prices, recipients or delivery records. No customer imports the service wallet for a new automatic order.

The contract records invoice attempt IDs and derives remaining capacity from actual outcomes: reservations, settlements and recipient acknowledgments consume the confirmation; only definitive failure releases it. This prevents a paid invoice from funding a later delivery. The worker scans pages of at most 50 orders, uses deterministic IDs and retains queued/uncertain/failed jobs as dispatch barriers. Failed automatic requests currently require operational review. These changes do not resolve physical-delivery disputes or semantic invoice duplicates.
